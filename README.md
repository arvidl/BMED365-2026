#### Course Overview
[BMED365](https://www.uib.no/en/course/BMED365) explores how artificial intelligence and computational tools are shaping modern biomedicine and healthcare. The course is offered in collaboration with the [Department of Biomedicine](https://www.uib.no/en/biomedisin) at the University of Bergen (UiB) and the Medical AI group at the [Mohn Medical Imaging and Visualization Center](https://mmiv.no) (MMIV).

The course consists of **two blocks**: Block 1 (January) is joint with ELMED219, and Block 2 (February) focuses on computational imaging and modeling.

#### What You Will Learn
This course provides practical knowledge in **computational thinking**, (bio)medical **image processing**, and the use of **machine learning** and **generative AI** in biomedicine and healthcare. You will learn to apply these skills in the context of **precision medicine** and **personalized medicine**, with a focus on generative AI, graph theory and patient similarity networks (PSN), quantitative imaging, and biomarkers. The course also addresses **ethical** and **regulatory** challenges, including data management and the EU AI Act, providing students with a balanced perspective on **explainable AI** (XAI), **trustworthy AI**, and **innovation** in medical and biomedical AI. Topics such as **neurosymbolic AI** and **agentic AI** will also be covered.

A more detailed course description with **topic list** can be found [here](./Course-description-topics/course-description-and-topics.pdf), and further specified by: [A-Ethics](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/A-Ethics/main.pdf), [B-ImageAnalysis](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/B-ImageAnalysis/main.pdf), [D-DeepLearning](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/D-DeepLearning/main.pdf), [E-Evaluation](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/E-Evaluation/main.pdf), [F-Skills](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/F-Skills/main.pdf), [G-GenerativeAI](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/G-GenerativeAI/main.pdf), [M-MachineLearning](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/M-MachineLearning/main.pdf), [N-GraphTheory](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/N-GraphTheory/main.pdf), [P-PSN](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/P-PSN/main.pdf), [S-Neurosymbolic](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/S-Neurosymbolic/main.pdf), [T-Trustworthy](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/T-Trustworthy/main.pdf), [X-XAI](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/X-XAI/main.pdf), [Y-ComputationalImaging](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/Y-ComputationalImaging/main.pdf), and [Z-ComputationalModeling](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/Z-ComputationalModeling/main.pdf).

#### Practical Applications
Through exercises and demonstrations, participants will work with practical applications, including analysis of MRI data, segmentation of medical images, building models for biomarker prediction, and exploring concepts such as [Patient similarity networks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11321866) and [multimodal data analysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-022-01981-2). The course also introduces large language models ([foundation models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundation_model)) and their potential use in healthcare.

#### [Skills and Tools](./Course-description-topics/Beamer/F-Skills/main.pdf)
Students will gain experience with Python programming, [Jupyter notebooks](https://docs.jupyter.org), modern AI tools such as [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com), [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com), and [Claude](https://claude.ai), **prompt** and **[context engineering](https://www.promptingguide.ai/guides/context-engineering-guide)**, as well as cloud-based data processing and [AI-assisted coding](https://github.com/resources/articles/ai/ai-coding-tools). Emphasis is placed on open science and reproducible research practices to prepare students for both academic and practical situations.

#### Team Project
The course includes a [team project](./Team-project) titled *"Precision Medicine and Quantitative Imaging in Glioblastoma"*. Students collaborate in interdisciplinary teams to develop a research plan (grant proposal sketch) that combines imaging technology, biomarkers, and machine learning. The project is joint with ELMED219 and the report is to be written in English.

#### Individual Project (Block 2)
In Block 2, students work on an [individual project](./Individual-project) defined by the student within the scope of the course. The project culminates in a digital speed poster presentation.

#### Why Take This Course?
BMED365 is an opportunity to acquire valuable skills and insights in the use of AI in medicine and healthcare, and with a special focus on computational imaging and modeling compared to ELMED219.

## Running Jupyter Notebooks

All notebooks in the course can be run **for free** in several ways. We recommend Google Colab as the main platform, but there are good alternatives.

### Recommended: Google Colab

[Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com) is a free, cloud-based service that lets you run Jupyter Notebooks directly in your browser – no installation required.

**Advantages:**
- No installation required
- Built-in AI assistance (Gemini)
- Free access to GPU/TPU for heavier computations
- Easy sharing and collaboration

**Requirement:** You need a **Google account**. If you don't have one, you can create one for free at [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com).

### Alternatives (without Google account)

If you don't have or don't want a Google account, there are several free alternatives:

| Platform | Account required? | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|----------|-------------------|------------|---------------|
| **[Kaggle Notebooks](https://www.kaggle.com/code)** | Yes (Kaggle account) | Free GPU, large ML community | Requires registration |
| **[Binder](https://mybinder.org)** | No | No registration, opens directly from GitHub | Can be slow to start, time-limited session |
| **[JupyterLite](https://jupyter.org/try-jupyter/lab/)** | No | Runs entirely in the browser | Limited functionality, not all packages |
| **Local installation** | No | Full control, works offline | Requires Conda/Python installation |

### Local Installation with Conda

For those who prefer to work locally, you can install Jupyter on your own machine:

```bash
# 1. Download and install Miniconda from https://docs.conda.io/en/latest/miniconda.html

# 2. Create the course environment
conda env create -f environment.yml
conda activate bmed365-2026

# 3. Start Jupyter
jupyter notebook
```

See [environment.yml](./environment.yml) for the complete list of packages used in the course.

> **Tip:** Regardless of which platform you choose, all course material is free and openly available. Choose the solution that works best for you!
